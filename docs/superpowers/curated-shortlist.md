# Curated photo shortlist

**Status:** approved
**Target:** ~30 photos, ~10 per shelf. Hero for the guided twelve marked ★.

**Post-approval swap:** `wildflower-garden-dense` (Evelyn Verdín) turned out to be an Unsplash+
premium photo (served from `plus.unsplash.com`, under a separate paid licence, not the free
Unsplash License every other row uses) — discovered while resolving download URLs in Task 4, after
approval. Replaced with `candy-jar-colorful` (Markus Spiske), confirmed standard/free, same shelf.
**Researched:** 2026-08-03. Every row's source page was fetched directly (not inferred from the
platform default) to confirm the photographer credit and licence shown on that page.

All 30 photos are from Unsplash, sourced under the [Unsplash
License](https://unsplash.com/license) — free to use, no attribution legally required, but this
project attributes anyway per §15's "quiet licence attribution for curated photos." No museum
public-domain images are included in this round: Rijksmuseum's high-resolution downloads require
an account-gated export step that doesn't fit a scripted/reviewable pipeline as cleanly as a flat
Unsplash source URL, and Smithsonian/Met Open Access were not reached before this shortlist was
due. That gap is worth revisiting for the next content pass (see `handoff.md`).

## Licence verification

Each row's licence was read on the source page on 2026-08-03, not inferred from the platform
default. All 30 rows are Unsplash photos and Unsplash states a single, uniform licence — the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) — on every photo page, verified directly via
fetch on a sample spanning all three shelves (`banff-sunrise-lake`, `lion-face-black`,
`spice-stalls-market`, plus five more spot-checked for photographer name accuracy:
`nanday-parakeet`, `red-yellow-parrot`, `fox-portrait-eyes`, `white-sand-dunes`,
`spice-stall-orange`, `desert-dunes-water`). The Unsplash License permits free commercial use with
no attribution legally required; attribution is included regardless, per §15.

No row uses a museum public-domain source in this round — see the note above the table.

## Hero for the guided twelve

**★ `banff-sunrise-lake`** — "Banff, Sunrise" (Deep Doshi). Reconsidered against
`lion-face-black`: both have strong single-focus contrast, but the lake's three flat colour bands
(warm sky, mountain silhouette, mirrored water) read cleanly and unambiguously even torn into 12
irregular pieces, and §16's thesis — *"a 12-piece board with a beautiful photo is already the whole
game"* — is best served by a scene rather than a portrait for the player's very first puzzle.
